# Suggested human review sequence

1. Assign an accountable service owner to confirm service names and provider relationships.
2. Confirm whether listed providers are direct providers; this demo does not capture fourth parties.
3. Review shared-provider flags against business impact, alternatives, contractual terms and existing contingency plans.
4. Record evidence, decisions, accountable owners and review dates in an approved system.
5. Revisit the map after material service, contract or architecture changes.

The script supplies no risk rating or prescribed treatment. Its flags are prompts, not findings. The fixture is synthetic; do not enter production or personal data.
